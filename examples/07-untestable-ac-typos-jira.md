## Problem Statement
Maintenance techs close work orders on paper, and the office types them in up to three days later. Residents keep calling to ask whether repairs were done.

## User Story
As a maintanence tech, I want to close work orders from my phone so that residents see the status right away.

## Acceptance Criteria
AC-1: The mobile screen should be user friendly.
AC-2: Closing a work order should be fast and intutive.
