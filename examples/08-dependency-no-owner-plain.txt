Status: Ready for Sprint
Story Points: 8
Assignee: L. Haddad

Problem Statement:
Residents pay rent through a portal that doesn't show late fees until after they pay, which causes disputes and refund requests every month.

User Story:
As a resident, I want to see any late fee before I submit my payment, so that I pay the right amount the first time.

Acceptance Criteria:
AC-1: Given a late fee applies, when the resident opens the payment page, then the fee and new total are shown before the Pay button.
AC-2: Given no late fee applies, when the resident opens the payment page, then no fee line is shown.

Dependencies:
Needs a change to the payment processor integration.
